Sustainability at Maxis

Creating shared value responsibly

Our sustainability framework focuses on three pillars — Planet, People and Governance. We enable equitable access to technology while managing environmental impact and upholding the highest standards of ethics.

Science-based targets Net-zero by 2050 Digital inclusion

Planet

Transitioning to renewable energy, enhancing energy efficiency across sites and deploying circular economy practices in devices and network hardware.

People

Building an inclusive workforce, enhancing digital skills for youths and SMEs, and fostering a culture of well-being and safety across all operations.

Governance

Robust board oversight, transparent disclosure and zero tolerance for corruption through strong controls and stakeholder engagement.

Communities

Empowering education and entrepreneurs via Maxis eKelas, digital maker programmes and targeted SME support in underserved areas.
